Physics behind the simulator

The plant — SLS-100

The SLS-100 (Single Loop Simulated) is a compact PWR of about 100 MWe: one RCS loop (core, RCP, U-tube SG, pressurizer), BOP, CVCS, ECCS, RHR, and RPS/ESF. Hot shutdown (Mode 4, 250 °F) through full power is one continuous plant.

Model in short: six-group point kinetics · nodal loop with real break locations · steam tables under everything · load coupled through the secondary · protection on instrument channels · degraded regimes open · no spatial core.

Core power and reactivity

  • Point kinetics, six delayed-neutron groups — one average power, no flux shape. Period and startup rate drive the nuclear instruments.
  • Reactivity terms: rods (S-curve bank worth), Doppler (fuel temperature), moderator from coolant density (not a fixed MTC), void, boron, xenon, and core excess.
  • I–Xe chain with burnout — whole-core only, no spatial oscillation.
  • Decay heat with power-history dependence — four decay groups, each relaxing toward its own equilibrium fraction of power.

Heat, pressure, and inventory

  • Lumped nodes (fuel, cladding hot node, coolant, secondary) with first-order heat transfer — not a TH systems code.
  • Pressurizer thermodynamics (heaters, spray, PORV / safeties); solid and empty ends are reachable.
  • Inventory from CVCS, ECCS, accumulators, leaks, and relief. Subcooling tracked; primary can void; RCP cavitation from suction margin.
  • Degraded regimes stay open — uncovered SG tubes cut heat transfer; voided primary and solid pressurizer are allowed states, not clamped out.

Secondary and electrical

  • SG mass-energy balance couples steam, feed, dumps, and isolation to primary heat removal.
  • Turbine / condenser / generator: load is a steam demand on the primary; loss of turbine or heat sink leaves energy in the plant.

Instruments and protection

Board quantities are lagged instrument channels (optional stuck/drift). Trips, interlocks, safeguards, and automatic controllers take those channel values — not true state. A failed transmitter misleads both the operator and the automation.

What is left out on purpose

  • No spatial neutronics or xenon tilt.
  • One loop, lumped nodes — no multi-loop or subchannel TH.
  • No containment / LOCA licensing stack (core-damage paths for training progression only).
  • Not a digital twin of a named unit.

Coefficients, step order, and declared departures: simulation-physics chapter in the in-sim manual, or the engine sources on GitHub (AGPL).
