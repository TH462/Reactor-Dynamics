Physics engine

Model form of the real-time PWR plant — what is integrated, how it is coupled, and what is left out. Written for readers who already know plant dynamics. Operator-facing plant description is on About.

Class: lumped-parameter behavioural plant · single-loop PWR · ~100 MWe / ~300 MWt · fixed 0.02 s explicit Euler · browser JavaScript · training fidelity, not licensing analysis.

Core, primary, pressurizer, steam generator, turbine-generator, and control/protection advance as one coupled system each step. Time acceleration runs more fixed steps per wall-clock second; it does not enlarge dt.

Neutronics

  • Point kinetics, six delayed-neutron groups (fixed U-235 constants). Prompt generation time and total delayed fraction are in the engine config. Constant source term for subcritical 1/M response.
  • Reactivity balance: core excess + rods (S-curve bank worth) + Doppler (fuel temperature) + moderator (coolant density, not a fixed MTC) + dissolved boron + xenon.
  • I–Xe chain with production, decay, and flux burnout. Whole-core inventory only — no spatial xenon oscillation.
  • Decay heat: two-term exponential with in-power production; post-scram heat depends on prior power history.

Thermal-hydraulics

  • Lumped nodes — fuel, cladding hot node, coolant, secondary; first-order heat transfer. Correlations, not steam tables or a two-phase property library.
  • Pressurizer — thermodynamic steam space with heaters, spray, PORV/safeties. Level derived from inventory and bubble; solid and empty ends are reachable.
  • Primary pressure — one dynamic reference (PZR) plus quasi-static loop distribution; RCP cavitation from suction subcooling.
  • Inventory and void — CVCS, ECCS, accumulators, leaks, relief. Inventory-driven and flux-driven void are separate states (bulk saturation vs core-exit boiling at high power).
  • Degraded regimes stay open — subcooling tracked, SG heat transfer falls as tubes uncover, primary can void, PZR can go solid. Those paths are intentional training content, not clamped out.

Secondary and electrical

  • SG mass-energy balance couples steam demand, main/aux feed, dumps, and isolation to primary heat removal.
  • Turbine, condenser, and generator close the BOP. Load is a steam demand on the primary; loss of turbine or heat sink leaves stored energy somewhere in the plant.

Instruments, protection, automation

Board quantities are lagged (and optionally failed) instrument channels. Trips, interlocks, safeguards, and automatic controllers take those channel values as inputs — not true state. Failed transmitters therefore mislead both the operator and the automation.

Deliberately not modeled

  • No spatial neutronics (no 3-D flux, no nodal power shape, no Xe tilt).
  • No multi-loop or subchannel TH; DNB/thermal limits are lumped, not hot-channel.
  • No containment engineering suite / LOCA licensing stack.
  • Not a digital twin of a named unit — composite ~100 MWe plant.

Detail is spent where operator-visible dynamics live. Full step order, coefficients, and declared departures are in the in-sim manual chapter on simulation physics, and in the engine sources on GitHub (AGPL).
