Roadmap

What is open now, and what is still in work.

The PWR plant — free play, operator manuals, and the live board — is open now. The items below are in progress. Dates are not promised; order can change.

Coming next

Training campaign

A guided path from first skills toward full operation: missions that watch the board and gate progress through real procedures, not click-through slides.

Scenarios

Instructor-led situations — one lesson each — from normal evolutions to casualties (including longer story sequences). In work.

Live checklists

Procedures that tick themselves off against the plant as it sits: you operate; the checklist grades the instruments. Aimed at free-play practice without a full scripted mission.

Procedure walkthroughs

Step-by-step follow mode in the Instructor panel: the same manual procedures, advanced one step at a time with the plant checked as you go. Related to live checklists, but a different way to run them.

BWR and RBMK control rooms

Boiling-water and graphite-channel plants already have physics engines in the project. Public control rooms for each — the same “operate the board” experience as the PWR — are not ready yet.

Available now

  • PWR free play, cold shutdown through full power
  • Operator manuals in the control room
  • Injected plant failures and free-play recovery
  • Offline single-file download
  • Physics write-up of the plant model

Progress also shows up in the changelog when something actually ships.
