Feedback & bug reports

Email is the way in now — there is no web form.

Write to reactordynamics@gmail.com. For a bug, say what you were doing, what you expected, and what the board showed. Inside the simulator, the 💬 button copies that address and can download a session diagnostics file you can attach.
