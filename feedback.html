Feedback & bug reports

Something broke, or something's wrong about the physics or procedures? Tell me.

Reporting a bug from inside the simulator is best. Click the 💬 button in the control room — it can attach your current session’s telemetry (plant readings and the commands you issued) so what you saw can be replayed exactly. This page is for general feedback without telemetry. Direct submission is coming soon; for now, the form packages your report into a file you can download and hold onto.
