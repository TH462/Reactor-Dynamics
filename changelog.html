Changelog

What has changed in the simulator, newest first.

This log begins with the public launch. The years of development before that aren’t listed — there is no point cataloguing changes to something nobody could yet use.

Offline download, a guided tour, and a reactor fitted to measured data

Alpha 1.11.0 30 July 2026

Version numbers restart here. Everything below was built and tested with a small group before the simulator was shown publicly, and the per‑release history has been folded into this single summary. The full engineering record is kept in the repository.

  • AddedThe whole simulator downloads as one file and runs offline. No install, no server — open it and it works, including on a machine that has never seen it before.
  • AddedA guided tour on first pause, an About page and a rebuilt public site with Physics and Roadmap pages, and a menu that works on a phone.
  • AddedThe board explains itself. Point at anything and it tells you what it is, what it is wired to, and the trap that catches people — and every description links into the operator’s manual.
  • AddedAutomatic controls now say what they are doing, including the reason one switched itself off. The feedwater control carries a status word, so a controller still lit AUTO but out of authority to correct says so instead of looking healthy while the level refuses to move.
  • AddedNew annunciators for diagnosing a leak and for safety injection accumulators left lined up during a cooldown, plus a reactor coolant pump seal leak to practise on — sized so every setting is one the plant can hold, making it a diagnosis exercise rather than an accident.
  • ChangedThe reactor’s response to temperature is fitted to measured plant data, and control and shutdown rod worths are the real measured values rather than estimates.
  • ChangedThe plant can heat itself from cold on pump heat alone, the way a real one does.
  • FixedThe pressurizer could not be filled solid by emergency injection — the exact trap Three Mile Island turned on. Indicated level stuck part‑way up the scale however much was injected; it now runs the full range and its high‑level trip is reachable.
  • FixedA by‑the‑book cooldown emptied all four safety injection accumulators, leaving the plant at cold shutdown with no passive injection left. The procedures now carry the isolation step.
  • FixedDiluting boron while the plant was still cold could take the reactor critical unexpectedly.
  • FixedAlarm bands follow the protection that is actually armed, so blocking a trip moves the band that shows it.
  • FixedThe low reactor coolant flow trip reads a real flow instrument rather than a hidden true value, so it can lag, drift and fail like any other channel.
