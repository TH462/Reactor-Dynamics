Cold shutdown, and the board stops lying about a correct plant

Alpha 1.7.2-rc4 3 September 2026
  • AddedMode 5, Cold Shutdown is a Free Play starting condition. The plant boots cold (122 °F) and depressurized, and heats up to Hot Standby on reactor-coolant-pump work with the reactor never critical.
  • FixedSafety injection actuates and the board now says so. It could actuate on a healthy plant during a heatup with nothing annunciating it and eccs_mode still reading standby.
  • AddedThe intermediate-range flux reactor trip is built, and TRIP BLOCKS no longer offers two blocks no operator has — the third row was drawing a rod stop's setpoint in a trip's place.
  • ChangedThe control bank is 627 steps, the sourced four-bank overlap program, and the flattening of its worth curve is bank overlap rather than a tuning constant.
  • FixedTwo boxes would not accept typing — the Pressurizer Pressure Setpoint and the Report a Problem form. A click that missed the field left the keyboard driving time acceleration instead.
  • ChangedMode 5 and Mode 4 boot with the turbine tripped and main feed secured, which is the lineup those modes actually hold.
  • FixedBoard bands read the running plant. The pressurizer level tile follows the live level program instead of a fixed full-power band, so an on-program Mode 5 level no longer paints 15 points below normal.
  • FixedOperator manuals and live checklists: the safety-injection accumulator cover gas is 665 psia (was 600 psi, the licence minimum), the heatup and cooldown accumulator windows read correctly, and the six live checklists are rewritten — each step is one action naming the board's own buttons, the reasoning sits one click below it, and four steps that quoted the retired plant's 200-step rod scale now give this plant's 627-step positions. Four manual chapters gave the P-10 permissive as 10 %; it is 8 % on this plant.

Playtest fixes and a faster board

Alpha 1.7.1 31 August 2026
  • FixedThe condenser cooling-water temperature box works. Vacuum follows the water temperature, and a warm enough lake now rings COND VAC LO (crossing ~76 °F).
  • FixedFrame rate and flicker under load. The strip chart no longer redraws unchanged frames and board animations run on a 12 Hz clock — render load cut roughly in half.
  • FixedThe pressurizer spray box shows the demand you set, not delivered flow — it no longer zeroes on a solid pressurizer or reads 100 % on a stuck valve.
  • FixedTrip-block rows match this plant's protection — dead rows removed, the safety-injection block added.
  • FixedThe Failures tab lists named failures for the PWR (was raw internal ids), and a refused injection is no longer shown as active.
  • FixedRod stop and turbine runback are indicated on the ΔT power tile, and the period readout no longer overlaps it.
  • ChangedAuxiliary feedwater manual START and throttle removed — the automatic level control runs auxiliary feed.
  • ChangedThe AUX SPRAY box is removed from the pressurizer card.

A new physics engine

Alpha 1.7.0 30 August 2026
  • AddedThe PWR runs on a rebuilt physics engine. Derived design basis, steam-table water properties throughout, and conservation laws the plant can be measured against.
  • ChangedThe plant now starts at Hot Shutdown (Mode 4, 250 °F) — Cold Shutdown is gone for now. Mode 5 is not modelled on the new engine. Four starting states: Hot Full Power, 50 % Power, Hot Standby, Hot Shutdown.
  • AddedAuxiliary pressurizer spray, on the pressurizer card. It lowers pressure with the reactor coolant pumps stopped, which the normal spray cannot do.
  • ChangedThe Three Mile Island level deception is emergent. Leave a relief valve stuck open and pressurizer level rises while the plant loses water — produced by the model, not scripted.
  • FixedEvery row of the instrument-failure panel works. 35 of its 50 instruments previously refused to fail.
  • FixedA tripped turbine can be re-latched. One scram used to end electrical generation for the rest of the session.
  • FixedBoard indications, alarm setpoints and control ranges read this plant's numbers rather than the previous engine's.
  • ChangedOperator manuals at Revision 17.

Cold shutdown starts with the rods in

Alpha 1.6.1 18 August 2026
  • ChangedCold shutdown now starts with the shutdown bank inserted. Withdrawing it is a step in the startup procedure. Loading Mode 5 and driving to Mode 5 now give the same plant.
  • ChangedThe turbine rolls at 10–15 % power, not below 5 %. The steam dumps already pass that flow, so total steam flow barely changes as the governor valves open.
  • FixedShutdown cooling can no longer be aligned during a safety injection. The same pumps do both jobs, and aligning mid-accident gave the full shutdown-cooling heat sink through heat exchangers that have no cooling in that lineup.
  • ChangedManuals at Revision 16. Shutdown margin is defined, and pressures are marked psia or psig.

The control room, rebuilt

Alpha 1.6.0 12 August 2026
  • ChangedThe strip chart is a stack of lanes. One lane per indication, each with its own scale, name and value, and a shared cursor that reads every lane at the same instant. A dashed line marks where the run began.
  • AddedSequence of events. A ribbon under the chart marks what happened and when — cyan for your actions, amber for the plant's. Trips and safety injections cross every lane; component events cluster into counted badges.
  • AddedChart settings. Every channel the plant publishes, listed with its current reading, and a choice per channel: trace the instrument, the physics, or both. Both draws them together in one lane so you can see how far the gauge is from the plant.
  • ChangedThe Instructor is a tab, and it keeps a transcript. Guidance and walkthrough steps accumulate and scroll instead of overwriting one another.
  • ChangedPlant & Mission opens on load and the plant is running when you close it. Checklists are ordered by relevance, launch from free play, and link to the manual section they came from.
  • ChangedIndications has Plant and Physics columns, and the disagreement flag now measures each channel's own noise instead of a fixed band — so a gauge is flagged when it is further out than it normally wanders.
  • AddedReport a Problem from the header, in one action, with the diagnostics it will send shown before you send them.
  • FixedFour plant behaviours. Safety injection now sheds the pressurizer heaters, ending a slow pressure oscillation after every loss-of-coolant accident; shutdown cooling no longer aligns itself into an unisolated break; full auxiliary feedwater cools the plant instead of banking the heat; free play starts with rod control in manual.

A protection function that never fired

Alpha 1.5.2 10 August 2026
  • FixedAutomatic steam line isolation never fired. On a steam line break the valves stayed open and you had to isolate by hand. It now closes on the rate of pressure drop, as the setpoint specifies, within a few seconds of the break.
  • FixedBug reports recorded almost nothing during fast transients. A report sent from an accident at high time acceleration captured a handful of readings instead of the event; it now records the peaks and troughs of every channel.
  • AddedContact offers GitHub issues alongside email. It opens in a new tab and the plant keeps running.

Every reading the plant produces, on one tab

Alpha 1.5.0 9 August 2026
  • AddedIndications tab, replacing Graph. All 84 of the plant's channels with their live values and a checkbox to trend any of them, grouped like the Physics tab. Status channels plot as on/off traces, and every row explains what it is on hover.
  • AddedSimulator performance readout on the Physics tab — where each update's time goes, physics against rendering, and whether the sim is keeping up. It is included automatically in bug reports.
  • ChangedFree play starts at the 50 % power preset, not Hot Full Power — there is somewhere to go in both directions.
  • AddedPhysics tab gained a plot column and 12 more rows; leak flow now reads in gpm.
  • ChangedThe PORV reads its demand signal, not the valve. Under a stuck-open relief valve, Indications shows it shut while Physics shows OPEN · STUCK — the Three Mile Island control room, on two tabs.
  • ChangedCondenser cooling water inlet: 35–85 °F (1.7–29.4 °C), default 60 °F (15.6 °C) — was 40–100 °F on an 80 °F day. A default day still makes rated vacuum and 100 MWe.
  • ChangedTurbine load raises are rate-limited to 30 %/min.
  • FixedThe strip chart's x-axis no longer jumps as it scrolls.

Hydrogen is real, and the containment fights back

Alpha 1.4.0 8 August 2026
  • AddedHydrogen. Overheated cladding generates it, it collects in containment, recombiners work the tail, and past 8 % it burns once — a TMI-2-style pressure spike. New H₂ gauge and annunciators (H2 HI, H2 BURN, RECOMB ON).
  • AddedContainment protection and heat removal. Spray at 30 psig, fan-cooler realign on safety injection, an unblockable 3.5 psig SI backup, and steam-line isolation — all automatic; board controls come with a later board redesign.
  • ChangedContainment pressure response re-worked. A station-blackout boil-off now pressurizes the building slowly and stays under the spray point — it used to pass design pressure on relief steam alone.
  • ChangedPressurizer level is a real inventory node. The gauge tracks the vessel's own liquid: large breaks empty it honestly, and the small-break level lift is now the documented real-plant class (IE Bulletin 79-06A).
  • ChangedAuxiliary feedwater starts on the sourced single signal — the 17 % low-low level that also trips the reactor; the invented 3-point offset is retired.
  • ChangedAuto steam-generator feed regulates to the programmed 65 % level, and the feed demand box reads NO FLOW when commanded feed is not being delivered.
  • FixedThe ADV valve's noisy-instrument failure did nothing; 14 instruments missing from the in-app manual data are restored.

Accidents run on a real clock

Alpha 1.3.0 7 August 2026
  • ChangedLoss-of-coolant events run at real rates. Break flow, safety injection, accumulators, relief and charging all move true fractions of reactor coolant inventory per second, so the gallons-per-minute readouts are literal. A stuck-open pressurizer relief valve with injection secured now reaches core damage in about 2 hours 20 minutes — the Three Mile Island timeline, where it used to take minutes.
  • ChangedThe relief valves are sized for this plant. The pressurizer PORV passes about 112 gpm and the code safeties 3.2 times that. Feed-and-bleed cooling is viable, and full safety injection out-runs one wide-open valve — which is why securing injection in 1979 was the decision that mattered.
  • ChangedA large break blows down toward the containment building instead of stalling at the remnant's boiling pressure: a full-size break bottoms at 116 psi (0.80 MPa) where it floored at 170 psi (1.17 MPa). Smaller breaks are unchanged.
  • FixedThe pressurizer level gauge no longer argues against a large break. It read 100 % at the moment the core top uncovered; with a hole in the loop the pressurizer now drains, while the Three Mile Island stuck-valve deception keeps its full strength on the paths where it is historical fact.
  • AddedCore-exit thermocouple. The subcooling margin reads the hotter of the loop bulk and a new core-exit channel, so it goes hard negative over an uncovering core instead of reporting comfort from injection-chilled water.
  • AddedChecklists check their prerequisites. A live checklist grades its procedure's preconditions against the plant and shows what the procedure expects against what the plant reads. It warns and never blocks, and clears itself when the plant is brought to where the procedure assumes.
  • ChangedA reflooded core can recover. Core damage is no longer latched as terminal while water is coming back — a partially melted core that is re-covered stabilises, as Three Mile Island's did. Unmitigated accidents still end the same way.
  • ChangedAtmospheric dump valve setpoint 1247 → 1272 psi (8.60 → 8.77 MPa), onto the sourced placement rule. A bottled steam generator now holds at 1276 psi (8.80 MPa) / 576.5 °F (302.5 °C).

The board stops flickering during transients

Alpha 1.2.2 6 August 2026
  • FixedElements blinking during large transients. Gauges, the trend chart, the alarm list and the clock were being rebuilt many times a second whether or not their values had changed, and could be caught mid-rebuild on screen.
  • FixedBubbles in the reactor, steam generator and pressurizer restarting. The bubble fields are reused instead of rebuilt, so they keep rising instead of snapping back to the start.
  • FixedVital gauge trends drawing outside their card, across the gauge band and onto the board, when a reading sat outside that gauge's scale. An off-scale reading now pins to the edge of its trace box.

A steadier board, and the atmospheric dumps start in auto

Alpha 1.2.1 6 August 2026
  • ChangedThe atmospheric dump valves start in AUTO. They hold a bottled steam generator at 1247 psi (8.60 MPa) instead of leaving it on its code safety valves. They still do not cool the plant on their own — lower the setpoint or open them for that.
  • FixedFlickering during large transients. Gauges, the trend chart, the alarm list and the clock no longer strobe when a lot is happening at once.
  • FixedAlarm acknowledgements that did not register. During an alarm flood a click could land on nothing. The alarm list can also be scrolled while alarms are coming in.
  • FixedThe six vital gauge trends were blank above 600× speed. Their window now follows the speed setting and they sample between updates.
  • ChangedThe vital gauge trends match the strip chart. They show a min/max band, hold a steady scale instead of rescaling every frame, and no longer drop brief spikes.
  • FixedThe turbine exhaust pipe ran underneath the turbine control card.
  • FixedSteam generator bubbles stopped partway up the water. They now rise to the surface and fade there.
  • ChangedManual corrections. The full-open cooldown rate is re-measured at about 630 °F/hr, and the steam dump section carries current numbers.

Containment, atmospheric dumps, and automatic steam line isolation

Alpha 1.2.0 6 August 2026
  • AddedA containment building. It has pressure, temperature and a sump, and a break or relief discharge pressurizes it. A steam generator tube rupture does not — that break bypasses containment.
  • AddedAtmospheric dump valves. You can cool the plant down with the condenser lost. They ship shut, and at full open they cool about three times faster than the 100 °F/hr limit.
  • AddedAutomatic main steam line isolation on high steam flow together with low steam pressure. It cannot be reopened until the generator re-pressurizes.
  • ChangedDecay heat is refitted to the published standard. Post-trip timings are longer: a station blackout reaches core damage at 2.6 hours, against under 2 before.
  • ChangedThe graph shows the full range a value reached, its time windows follow the speed setting, and its resolution no longer depends on speed.
  • FixedA leaking plant that goes water-solid now reaches its relief valve instead of filling to the model ceiling.
  • FixedA steam line break no longer empties the generator to the model floor. It is a mass flow now, so the generator recovers to its safety band.
  • AddedThe release number is shown beside the ALPHA badge on every page.

Cooldown rate indication, turbine stop valves, and a run of physics fixes

Alpha 1.1.0 5 August 2026
  • AddedRCS cooldown and heatup rate meter, with high-rate annunciators at ±100 °F/hr.
  • AddedTurbine stop valves. A trip now shuts steam off in a fraction of a second, separately from the governor.
  • ChangedFeedwater carries heat. Overfeeding overcools the plant, and auxiliary feedwater is a real heat sink.
  • FixedSteam generator code safety valves act on steam pressure itself. A failed pressure transmitter can no longer stop them lifting.
  • FixedPressurizer level no longer freezes at a normal reading as the plant heats. It rises to the top and the relief valve lifts.
  • FixedA break stops cooling the plant once the coolant stops boiling.
  • FixedA stopped reactor coolant pump no longer heats the coolant.
  • ChangedTwo limits are stated in the manual: steam line isolation is a manual action, and the steam dump goes to the condenser only.

Pressurizer, relief and the control board

Alpha 1.0.1 5 August 2026
  • FixedA water-solid reactor coolant system now repressurizes. Emergency injection into a full plant lifts the relief valve instead of vanishing.
  • FixedLosing coolant now moves pressure and subcooling margin, not just pressurizer level.
  • FixedA break weakens as the plant depressurizes. Closing the pressure difference reduces leak flow, and an empty vessel stops discharging.
  • FixedThe Three Mile Island scenarios and campaign play through again, and the decision point is now the one the 1979 crew faced: restore the injection that was just secured.
  • ChangedThe tube-rupture procedure secures injection before depressurizing — without it, walking the pressure setpoint down does nothing.
  • ChangedThe diagram shows delivered flow, not commanded flow. Pumps, valves and pipes stop animating when nothing is moving.
  • ChangedPipe colours read the plant. Letdown, charging, emergency injection, accumulator and auxiliary feed lines take live temperatures; coolant green and orange are darker.
  • AddedPressurizer spray flow indication and a main feedwater RESTORE control on the steam generator feed card.
