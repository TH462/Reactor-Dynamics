Changelog

What has changed in the simulator, newest first.

This log begins with the public launch. The years of development before that aren’t listed — there is no point cataloguing changes to something nobody could yet use.

Awaiting public launch

This build is pre‑alpha. It has no version number yet — it identifies itself by build SHA in the footer, which is the number to quote in a bug report. The changelog starts at Alpha 1.0.0 on public launch day; until then, changes are tracked in the repository.

The first release covers the pressurised water reactor. The RBMK and BWR are out of scope for it and will follow later.

Reactor Dynamics is an educational simulation — not engineering software, and no substitute for real operator training.
