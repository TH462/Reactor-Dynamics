Changelog

What has changed in the simulator, newest first.

This log begins with the public launch. The years of development before that aren’t listed — there is no point cataloguing changes to something nobody could yet use.

Hands-on TMI-2, a calmer board, and a smoother ride

Alpha 1.0.1 23 July 2026
  • ChangedTMI-2, Part 1 is now hands-on. You work the controls the way the night-shift crew did — securing injection, isolating the stuck relief valve — instead of only watching it play out. The stuck valve, its hot tailpipe, and the maintenance tag are all visible on the board now.
  • ChangedCalmer, more honest gauges. Indication noise is cut in half, and boration/dilution now moves power at the pace of the actual (slowly‑mixing) boron rather than jumping ahead of the reading. Control rods are a touch gentler near startup.
  • AddedKeyboard rods and clearer controls. Drive the control rods with the ↑/↓ keys, and every clickable control now glows when you hover it. The generator‑load and boron arrows step by 1 (MW / ppm) for fine trimming.
  • FixedNo more flicker or stuck rods. The changing numbers and strip chart no longer strobe after a few minutes of play, and holding an arrow key no longer leaves the rods drifting after you let go.

Found a bug, or want to suggest something? The feedback page comes straight to me, and there is a 💬 button inside the simulator that can attach your session so I can see what actually happened.

Reactor Dynamics is an educational simulation — not engineering software, and no substitute for real operator training.
