Changelog

What has changed in the simulator, newest first.

This log begins with the public launch. The years of development before that aren’t listed — there is no point cataloguing changes to something nobody could yet use.

Steam flow on the board, trip on turbine trip

Alpha 1.7.0 27 July 2026
  • AddedSTEAM FLOW readout. Sits directly above SG FEED RATE on the same scale, so holding feedwater in MANUAL is a matter of matching two numbers instead of chasing level. It reads total main‑steam‑line flow, so it stays live with the turbine tripped and the steam dump carrying the plant.
  • AddedReactor trip on turbine trip. Above about 50 % power a turbine trip now trips the reactor. Below that it is bypassed automatically, and the plant rides the trip out on the steam dump.
  • AddedLOAD IMBAL annunciator. Lights when reactor power and turbine load diverge. Reducing power on rods alone used to overcool the plant from 304 °C to 130 °C with nothing on the board saying so.
  • AddedRelease version by the logo in the control room.
  • ChangedStartup checklist rebuilt around the 1/M approach. Six plotted points instead of three, so the prediction converges on criticality instead of running some eighty rod steps long; new steps put feedwater in automatic and block the startup trips above P‑10. The approach steps now grade on measured source‑range counts rather than reactivity.
  • ChangedRod insertion limit is power‑dependent. Not applicable below 5 % power and ramping to 70 % withdrawn at full power, instead of a flat floor that lit ROD INS LIMIT during every startup.
  • ChangedSteam‑line breaks now have a location. The standard break is downstream of the isolation valve and closing the MSIV ends it; a new break inside containment cannot be isolated at all.
  • ChangedLarger PWR board with elastic right‑hand columns, removing the dead space at wide window sizes.
  • FixedIndication noise is set per readout. One global setting had feed and steam flow dancing at ten times their display resolution while power and output were unnaturally still. Display smoothing has been removed, so a needle now lags only as far as its instrument does.
  • FixedAutomatic feedwater control could not see the steam dump. With the turbine offline or tripped it read almost no steam flow and commanded no feed while the generator boiled down.
  • FixedAuxiliary feedwater holds level in the green band. It used to settle at 21 %, inside the amber.
  • FixedFour readouts were showing fiction. Accumulator pressure was a hard‑coded number and now falls as the tank drains, SG FEED RATE showed pump demand rather than measured flow, the condensate polisher always read NORMAL, and net reactivity printed “+−0 pcm”.
  • FixedStartup no longer overshoots to about 20 % power. The startup rate alarm and the rod withdrawal block are now set where they catch a fast ascent.
  • FixedRod speed on the first step. A bank left part‑way through a step took its next step almost immediately, whatever speed was selected.
  • FixedStrip charts keep each trace in its own lane, hold still when the plant does, and no longer turn white.
  • FixedHovering a checklist step no longer pulls panels in front of the reactor vessel.

Anonymous visitor counts

Alpha 1.6.1 24 July 2026
  • AddedPage‑view analytics. The site now counts visits, using the host’s cookieless analytics — page address, referring link, and country/device/browser. It sets no cookies and cannot track you across sites, and nothing you do inside the simulator is recorded.
  • ChangedPrivacy page updated to match. It now states exactly what is collected, instead of saying nothing is.

1/M startup checklist, checklist highlights

Alpha 1.6.0 24 July 2026
  • AddedChecklist highlights. Hovering a step in a live checklist glows the controls and readouts it uses.
  • ChangedRebuilt Mode 3 → Mode 1 startup checklist. Twelve steps that walk the 1/M plot approach to criticality, the source‑range → intermediate‑range handoff, and levelling off before the turbine goes on line.
  • ChangedProcedures list is a menu again. Each procedure shows compactly; expand a card to preview its steps.

Boron trend, 30-minute chart history

Alpha 1.5.0 24 July 2026
  • AddedBoron trend. Boron (ppm) is now a plottable graph option.
  • Changed30 minutes of history. The trend chart records 30 minutes; the time window sets how much is shown.
  • ChangedWider trends when the panel is hidden. Hiding the side panel widens the trend/alarms column into the freed space.
  • ChangedProactive trip blocks. A blockable trip can be blocked any time it isn’t already tripping; a block you set by hand stays until you clear it.

Three-column control room

Alpha 1.4.0 24 July 2026
  • ChangedThree-column layout. Plant diagram and vitals on the left, trend chart and alarms in the middle, simulator panel on the right. Larger diagram.
  • AddedHide the side panel. The ⛶ button hides the simulator panel and moves the time controls onto the trend/alarms column.
  • ChangedAuto-scaling trend chart. Each line scales to its own range; faint gridlines added.
  • ChangedGenerator load starts in Manual. Mode 1 free-play now begins in Manual rather than load-follow.

Fine rod control, timed pressurizing, startup checklists

Alpha 1.2.0 23 July 2026
  • ChangedFine-step control bank. 912 steps of travel; about 1.5 cents of reactivity per tap near critical. Old saves migrate.
  • ChangedPressurizing takes real time. A 350‑to‑600 psi setpoint step climbs over about 90 seconds.
  • AddedMode 5 → Mode 3 heatup checklist. Pumps, pressurization, instrument handoff, approach to criticality, dilution to Hot Standby.
  • ChangedStartup checklist runs to Mode 1. Carries past criticality across the 5 % boundary and puts the generator on line.
  • Fixed“Cold to Power” completion. The arrival check now declares Mode 1 reliably.

Xenon trend, clearer readouts

Alpha 1.1.0 23 July 2026
  • AddedXenon trend. Xenon (% of equilibrium) is now plottable on the strip chart.
  • ChangedVital gauges match the board. Same green/amber/red readout colors as the diagram.
  • ChangedAuto-controlled inputs read grey. Cyan means the value is yours to set.

Hands-on TMI-2, calmer board

Alpha 1.0.0 23 July 2026
  • ChangedTMI-2 Part 1 is hands-on. Secure injection and isolate the stuck relief valve yourself; the stuck valve, hot tailpipe, and maintenance tag are on the board.
  • ChangedCalmer gauges. Indication noise halved; boration/dilution moves power at the boron mixing rate. Gentler rods near startup.
  • AddedKeyboard rods and finer controls. Arrow keys drive the rods; controls highlight on hover; generator-load and boron arrows step by 1 MW / 1 ppm.
  • FixedNo strobing or drifting rods. Numbers and strip chart no longer strobe after a few minutes; rods stop when you release the key.

Found a bug, or want to suggest something? The feedback page comes straight to me, and there is a 💬 button inside the simulator that can attach your session so I can see what actually happened.
