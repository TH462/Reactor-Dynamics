Learn on a real plant model

The left side is the plant — operate rods, pumps, valves, feed, and the turbine there. The right column is the simulator: time, the session bar, the reference tabs and the Instructor. The System Scanner is the line under the board. Same high-fidelity physics whether you are learning the basics or practicing failures.

Where to start

Play starts the clock — the ▶ button flashes whenever the plant is stopped. F11 goes fullscreen, which suits the board.
Instructor (right column) — free-play coaching and, later, guided voices. Use − to minimize it.
Checklists (first tab) — interactive procedures that check themselves off the instruments.
Manual — full operator reference and written procedures.
Plant & Mission (the bar under the clock) — starting condition, courses and reset.
System Scanner (the line under the board) — hover anything for what it is and how to use it.

Colors on this Physics Diagram

Green — running / normal.  Yellow / amber — abnormal or alarm caution.  Red — problem or automatic protection (trip).  Cyan — you can type or click here.  Grey numbers — OK but not ideal, no alarm yet.

Watch what moves together

This plant is about the couplings: drop the generator load and power follows it down while Tavg rises; pressurizer level tracks Tavg, not inventory; the steam generator is the primary's only heat sink. Change one thing and watch what answers — that is the whole exercise.

Instruments lag, and can fail

You operate through instruments, and protection reads those same instruments — with lag, noise, and, in failure scenarios, a channel that has stopped reporting honestly. That is how real plants are run. Cross-check a doubtful reading against the couplings above; they keep holding when one gauge stops.

Rewind & speed

Rewind (by the strip chart) restores an earlier plant state so you can try again. Speed runs the same physics faster; optional fast-forward dropout drops you back to 1× when something needs attention.

Keyboard

Space — play / pause · 1–5 — speed · A — ack all alarms · M — manual · ? — this guide · Esc — close / cancel
