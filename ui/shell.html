❓ How this control room works

The board is quiet on purpose

A healthy plant is a dark board. Color appears only when something needs you: amber means caution, red means alarm. An empty alarm panel is itself information.

You operate through instruments — and instruments can lie

Every number on this board comes from a sensor that lags, drifts, and can fail. The plant's protection systems read the same sensors you do. At Three Mile Island a valve indicator read closed while the valve was stuck open — this simulator will do that to you, on purpose, with no visual tell. Cross-check your indications.

Where everything lives

⚛️ Mission line (top right, under the clock) — switch reactors, start the training campaign, scenarios, or procedure walkthroughs.
📖 Manual — the Operator's Manual: controls, indications, setpoints, normal values, and followable procedures.
⏪ Rewind (by the strip chart) — go back in time. Failure is never Game Over; rewind and try a different path. In free play, press it and then click the moment you want on the strip chart — every checkpoint in the window becomes a jump target; in a scenario or walkthrough it steps back one checkpoint.
System Scanner (bottom right) — hover or tap anything on the board and the scanner tells you what it is and what it's for.

Keyboard shortcuts

Space — play / pause · 1–5 — speed steps · A — acknowledge all alarms · M — manual · ? — this guide · Esc — close / cancel
