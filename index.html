What makes it different

The instruments can lie

You never see the true state of the plant — only instrument readings, which lag, drift, and fail. At Three Mile Island a valve indicator read “closed” while the valve was stuck open. Here, that gap is the whole point.

Real reactor physics

Point kinetics with delayed neutrons, xenon transients, decay heat, two-phase steam generators, and a full balance of plant from the core to the grid.

Guided campaigns

An instructor watches the board and gates your progress through real procedures — startup, power maneuvers, casualty response — with commercial-format operator manuals to study.

Break it on purpose

Inject stuck valves, failed sensors, and pump trips, then diagnose the casualty from a board that isn’t telling you the whole truth.

About

Reactor Dynamics is an educational simulator built to teach how nuclear plants actually behave — including the conditions behind Three Mile Island, Chernobyl, and Fukushima. Read more →

Reactor Dynamics is an educational simulation — not engineering software, and no substitute for real operator training.
