What you get

Learn plant operation

Sit a real board without a nuclear background. Pressure, temperature, level, rods, boron, steam, feed, turbine, and alarms — and the order of a startup from cold shutdown to power.

A whole plant, modeled

High-fidelity coupled physics: core and reactivity feedbacks, decay heat, pressurizer, steam generator, feedwater, turbine, and grid. A load change or a trip has consequences you can reason about.

Practice real work

Load changes, trips, recovery, and injectable failures — stuck valves, pump trips, sensor problems — on the same physics you learned on. You operate through instruments, the way a plant is actually run.

Guided training

An instructor watches the board and gates your progress through real procedures — startup, power maneuvers, casualty response — with commercial-format operator manuals in the room.
