Run a nuclear power plant
from your browser.

Real reactor physics, with guided walkthroughs that take you from cold shutdown to full power and back. Nothing to install — it all runs locally in your browser.

▶  Enter the Control Room
🚧 Alpha · desktop or laptop only — not for phones or tablets yet
🧪 PWR2 physics rebuild — dev preview (true values, no instruments)

The live plant is the SLS-100, a compact single-loop PWR (~100 MWe) — cold shutdown to full power, with procedures and failures on the same model. Start with About for what this is and how to approach it.

What’s here

Plant operation

Run the board: rods, boron, pressurizer, feed, steam, turbine, and alarms. Learn the order of a startup and how to hold the plant online — the what operators do. Free play: every start condition, every control live.

Plant dynamics

Core, primary, steam generator, and turbine are one coupled model. A load change or a trip has consequences you can reason about — the why behind the board. Model form and limits are on the Physics page.

Casualties and recovery

Trips, stuck valves, pump trips, sensor failures, tube leaks, loss of heat sink — inject them yourself and work the recovery. Same plant every session; an easier run is a gentler situation, not a simpler model.

Operator’s manual

A commercial-format manual set for this plant, open in the control room: controls and indications, normal and emergency procedures, alarm response, setpoints and limits, and a glossary.

Reactor Dynamics is an educational simulation — not engineering software, and no substitute for real operator training.
Found a bug, or something that does not match plant behavior? Open a GitHub issue — public and tracked, and you can read what is already known before you write. Anything else: reactordynamics@gmail.com.
