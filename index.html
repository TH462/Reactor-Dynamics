What makes it different

Reactivity you can feel

The core runs on point-kinetics with six delayed-neutron groups, and it pushes back: Doppler and moderator-temperature feedback, boron worth, and xenon poisoning all move the reactivity in real time. Watch net reactivity and reactor period as you approach criticality, and feel the coefficients fight — or amplify — every move you make.

A whole plant, modeled

Decay heat, two-phase steam generators, pressurizer level and pressure control, and a full balance of plant from the core through the turbine to the grid — coupled thermal-hydraulics where every valve, pump, and rod has a real, physical consequence.

Guided campaigns

An instructor watches the board and gates your progress through real procedures — startup, power maneuvers, casualty response — with commercial-format operator manuals to study.

Break it on purpose

Inject stuck valves, failed sensors, and pump trips, then diagnose the casualty from a board that isn’t telling you the whole truth.

About

Reactor Dynamics is an educational simulator built to teach how nuclear plants actually behave — including the conditions behind Three Mile Island, Chernobyl, and Fukushima. Read more →

Reactor Dynamics is an educational simulation — not engineering software, and no substitute for real operator training.
