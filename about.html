About Reactor Dynamics

Reactor Dynamics is a nuclear power plant simulator that runs in your browser. It is meant for learning how a plant is run, and for practicing the same work later — startups, load changes, trips, and failures — on one continuous model.

Nothing to install. Desktop or laptop only for now.

What it is for

Most people never sit a control room. Licensed training tools are hard to get. Toy sims often drop the physics until the plant cannot teach a real procedure. This project tries to sit between those two: plain enough to start without a nuclear background, and detailed enough that a load change or a stuck valve still has consequences you can follow.

It is not a game about scores. It is a plant you operate.

Who might use it

  • People new to nuclear who want the basics done carefully
  • Students and instructors who want a plant on the screen, not only slides
  • Anyone who wants to practice maneuvers or failures without a full training center

What you can do

Bring a cold plant up: pressurize, start the pumps, heat to hot standby, approach criticality, raise power, connect the generator, and hold steady. Operator manuals are in the control room. Free play leaves you on the board; guided missions (when offered) walk a path for you.

You can also change load, adjust boron, run feed and level control, trip and recover, and inject failures — stuck valves, pump trips, bad sensors, loss of heat sink. Beginners and harder sessions use the same plant model.

What is playable today

A compact pressurized water reactor (PWR), roughly 100 MWe, one coolant loop. Cold shutdown through full power is one continuous path. Two other reactor types (boiling-water and graphite-channel) have physics engines in the project; their public control rooms are not ready yet.

What you see on the board

You work from instrument readings — lagged, noisy, and able to fail — not a god-view of every true number. That is how real boards work, and it is how some of the hardest lessons (including historical accidents) actually happen. The plant still runs underneath; you have to operate through the panel.

How it runs

Ordinary web pages and JavaScript. No account required for the simulation itself. Open the site or a local copy and go into the control room.

Contact

Questions, bugs, or “this does not match plant behavior”? Email reactordynamics@gmail.com. In the simulator, the Contact control gives you that address and can download session diagnostics to attach.

Reactor Dynamics is an educational simulation. It is not a substitute for licensed operator training and must not be used to operate, design, or analyze a real nuclear facility.
